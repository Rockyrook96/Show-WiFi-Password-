​I build open-source hardware, low-level firmware, and security tooling at the intersection of IoT, RF systems, and robotics.
​As the creator of Badrabbit, I take projects from concept to execution—handling schematic design, multi-layer PCB layout, firmware architecture, and host UI integration.
​Key Areas of Focus
​Hardware & Firmware Engineering: End-to-end design, custom microcontrollers, RTOS, and protocol integration.
​Wireless & RF Security: Protocol analysis and security research across BLE, Wi-Fi, Sub-GHz, and RC control systems.
​Offensive Security & Malware Research: Crafting proof-of-concept exploits, reverse engineering binaries, and studying malware architecture to better understand hardware and firmware attack surfaces.
​Developer Education: Creating open-source documentation, tools, and technical content to empower other hardware hackers and researchers.
​Tips for Framing Security/Malware Research:
​Context Matters: Using terms like "Offensive Security Research," "Proof-of-Concept Exploit Development," or "Malware Architecture & Reverse Engineering" helps communicate your technical depth clearly to researchers, employers, and collaborators.
​Focus on Purpose: Highlight that the research fuels hardware security, defense, and developer education.
